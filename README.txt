gtclippper.fyi - brand assets

colours
  cream    #ffedd2
  black    #0d0d0d
  fuchsia  #f5288b

letterforms
  drawn from scratch, geometric rounded (poppins / nunito adjacent)
  stroke 32 - x-height 118 - bowl ratio 0.97 - round terminals
  middle p replaced by a magnifier: fuchsia lens, handle running down-left

folders
  lockup/     full wordmark. -primary is the default. -cream for dark
              backgrounds. -on-cream / -on-black have baked backgrounds.
              -solid-* are true single colour for engraving or embroidery.
  icon/       the magnifier p alone, same treatments.
  app-icon/   512 rounded tiles, mark at 60% to clear the ios corner mask.
  favicon/    small-size variant: shorter handle, thicker stroke.
  png/        rasterised exports.

use favicon/favicon-mark.svg for <link rel=icon> where you can.
